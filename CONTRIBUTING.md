# Feedback and contributions

Tawjihi Together is intended to help real students preparing for Tawjihi.

You can help by:
1. Reporting a factual error or broken interaction.
2. Suggesting a clearer explanation or a useful revision feature.
3. Testing a resource and sharing what was confusing or helpful.

When reporting an issue, include the subject, unit, section or question, and a short explanation. Do not include personal student information.

Before contributing content, make sure you have permission to share it. Prefer original explanations, summaries, and practice questions over reproducing copyrighted textbook pages or restricted answer keys.
