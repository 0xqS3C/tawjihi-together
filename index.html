Why Tawjihi Together?

Built around students helping students.

Tawjihi preparation can be demanding. This initiative aims to turn study material into interactive resources that help students practise, understand difficult topics, and return to questions they find challenging. It will grow through student feedback.

Created by a Jordanian Tawjihi student · Free learning resources · Content is being reviewed and improved.
